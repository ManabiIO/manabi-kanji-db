admission. Modern educational drawings remain a separate kind of material.

is being applied to these images.

## Selection boundaries

The deliberately checked common pictorial subjects are 日/月/水/火/人/目/鳥/馬,
using the individually named Commons oracle, bronze and seal SVG files. A file
name or the Commons page-text license does not establish image permission.
The retained Sears-derived transcriptions require an image-specific Sears reference and the
individual file's public-domain declaration/Sears permission. Generic links to
Sinica, Chinese Etymology or other databases in a template's “available sources”
list do not establish that the displayed asset came from them. No external
database is downloaded or incorporated.

The following exclusions concern the checked fixed files, not an assertion that
no reusable source for a subject exists:

These are modern transcriptions of script forms, not photographs of excavated
objects. Commons script classifications and Sears image identifiers support the
labels, not a dated lineage. No educational drawing is promoted into this class,
no formation role is inferred from it, and no compound inherits its component's
historical artwork.

The selected formats for this deliberate subject set include root paths, flat
editor layers, a plain translated group, absolute and relative cubic commands,
SVG 1.0/1.1 fixed canvases, explicit/default black fill and the two inert RDF
namespace spellings. No numerical quota determined selection. The 24 named
oracle/bronze/seal descriptions were checked individually; exclusions above
remain specific to that set. This is a useful selected teaching set, not complete
historical coverage or a claim to have exhausted Commons or palaeographic study.
