# JMdict and JmdictFurigana vocabulary evidence

JMdict is maintained by the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group and contributors. The EDRDG dictionary license is Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0. The overlay cites the 2026-11-27 English-edition XML snapshot for entry IDs and selected legal spelling and reading pairs. [EDRDG dictionary license](https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html)

JmdictFurigana is maintained by Doublevil and contributors and derives its vocabulary from JMdict. Its data license adopts JMdict’s Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike license. The overlay checks the full spelling and reading against the 2.3.1+2026-10-25 release, preserves original row selectors, and uses a span only when it covers exactly the target character. [JmdictFurigana data license](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Doublevil/JmdictFurigana/2.3.1%2B2026-10-25/README.md#licence) · [Pinned release](https://github.com/Doublevil/JmdictFurigana/releases/tag/2.3.1%2B2026-10-25)

ManabiDictionaries Kanji Explanation v26 contributors selected the starting vocabulary. The new teaching sentences cite the donor’s selection and the two external evidence records at field scope. The source registry records the exact snapshot hashes, versions, and attribution.
